Salary Heads

Salary heads are components that constitute Pay Structures. A salary head could be an earning, which is paid to an employee, or a deduction, which is recovered from his/her salary. The value of these Pay Heads could be either fixed or variable, for each Payroll period.

To access this module click on: Human Resource Module ->Navigate ->Operations ->Salary ->Salary Heads

Creating a new Salary Head

1\. Click on the create button and the following screen will open.

Take note of the salary heads circled in red.

2\. Select the type of salary head appropriately, the appropriate earning or deduction from the drop down menu of options.

3\. Link the salary head to a product. For Earnings, the product should be the ordinary savings, by clicking on the product look up icon and select the correct product to be linked to the salary head.

How To Edit A Salary Head

To edit a salary head, identify the salary head to be edited then click on the edit icon on extreme left of the salary head to open the edit salary head screen as below :

Salary Heads

Salary Heads fig.5

Make the necessary changes to the salary head entries i.e the Product, Name and G/L Account then click on the update button to save the changes into the system.

On successful completion you will get a pop up message confirming that the operation was successful.
