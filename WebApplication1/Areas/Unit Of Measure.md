Unit of Measure

You can create units of measurement for your inventory , using the unit of measure module. You may create custom units of measurement or standard units of measurement which you will later use to quantify assets and inventory.

To access this sub module click on: Control Module -> Navigate -> Setup -> Unit of Measure

How to edit a unit of measurement

You may want to edit a unit of measure later on without deleting the existing unit of measure, to edit a unit of measure do the following.

Click on the edit icon next to the unit of measure you want to edit. The edit icon looks like this .
