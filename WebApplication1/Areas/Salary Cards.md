Salary Cards

This module Links Employees to their respective salary groups.

To access this module click on Human Resource Module -> Navigate -> Operations -> Salary -> Salary Cards.

To link an employee to his/her salary card, click on the create button.

The following window will open:

Salary Cards

Salary Cards fig.1

1\. Select the employee that you wish to link up to their salary card by clicking on the lookup icon .

2\. Select their salary group by clicking on the salary group lookup icon and selecting the correct salary group.

3\. Enter the amount that the employee is tax exempted.

4.Enter the employee's Insurance relief amount.

5\. Enter remarks accordingly.

put a tick mark on the check box if the salary card is tax exempted

put a tick mark on the check box if the salary card is locked

6\. Click on the create button to save the changes.

Note: You are to repeat the above procedure for all Employees.

To confirm the Entry of the Employees and their various Job Groups, click on the Salary Cards, and then click on Refresh Button to see all the entries.

Click on the edit icon to edit (change) employees job group.

How to edit a salary card

The following windows open when click on the edit icon on the employee salary card listings.

Salary Cards

Salary Cards fig.2

1\. Identify the salary card to be edited.

2.The employee details will appear under the salary head button.If nothing is displayed click on the reset button on the far right of the window.

To modify :

Tax Exemption - Enter the new amount to be exempted in this field.

Insurance Relief - Enter the new amount of insurance relief to be enforced on the salary card.

Remarks - Enter the new remarks associated with the salary card.

3.Highlight on the salary head you intend to modify, at the bottom of the grid you will see the following

- Value type:Showing whether the amount is on fixed amount or percentage rate
- Group Value The total value attached to the salary head
- Card value:Showing the amount attached for that particular employee,you can modify individual employee salary head amount using this option.

2\. Make changes appropriately.

3\. Click on the UPDATE button to save changes.

On successful completion you will get a pop up message confirming that the operation was successful.
