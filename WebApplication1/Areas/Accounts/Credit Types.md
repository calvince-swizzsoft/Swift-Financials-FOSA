Credit Types

Use this sub-module to set up the various Credit Types for example; salary, dividends, casual workers pay etc.

In this sub-module you can also tag applicable charges to that Credit Type, attach loan products, and also attach investment products that will be applicable to that credit type.

How to Create a Credit Type

1\. Click on the CREATE Button, to open the following screen:

Credit Types fig 8.

2\. On this Create screen, fill in all the required data in all fields:

Name - Enter the name of the Credit Type to be created

G/L Account - Click on the lookup icon to select the appropriate G/L Account for that Credit Type .

3\. Transaction Ownership - Select the appropriate type of transaction ownership from the drop down list, as shown in this figure.

4\. Applicable Charges - select by clicking on all the applicable charges. for example if its a salary processing Credit Type, the applicable charges can be "salary processing commission"

5\. Applicable direct debits - select the applicable direct debits.

6\. Attached Loan Products - Select Loan products that are to tagged to this Credit Type; then click on the next button, attached loan products and select by clicking on all loan products that are to be attached to this Credit Type.

7\. Concession-Exempt Loan Products - select the concession exempt loan products that are to be attached to this credit type.

8\. Attached Investment Products - that are to tagged to this Credit Type; then click on the next button, attached investment products and select by clicking on all investment products that are to be attached to this Credit Type.

9\. Attached Savings Products - select the savings products that are to be attached to this credit type.

When done click on the CREATE Button to complete this task.

On successful completion you will get a pop up message confirming that the operation was successful.

How to Edit a Credit Type

1\. First Identify the Credit Type to be edited

2\. On that Credit Type that's been identified, at the far left, double click on the edit icon to open the following screen: (in this case we are editing Milk Payout credit type).

Credit Types fig 9.

On this Edit screen, modify all the required data in all fields:

3\. Name - If it's necessary you can modify / edit the name of the already existing Credit Type

4\. G/L Account - Click on the lookup icon to select and change the G/L Account appropriately for new / modified Credit Type .

5\. Transaction Ownership - Select /modify the type of transaction ownership from the drop down list, as shown in this figure.

6\. Applicable Charges - Modify/select by clicking on all the applicable charges. for example if its a salary processing Credit Type, the applicable charges can be "salary processing commission"

7\. Applicable direct debits - Modify/select the applicable direct debits.

8\. Attached Loan Products - Modify/Select Loan products that are to tagged to this Credit Type; then click on the next button, attached loan products and select by clicking on all loan products that are to be attached to this Credit Type.

9\. Concession-Exempt Loan Products - Modify/select the concession exempt loan products that are to be attached to this credit type.

10\. Attached Investment Products - Modify the investment products that are to tagged to this Credit Type; then click on the next button, attached investment products and select by clicking on all investment products that are to be attached to this Credit Type.

Attached Savings Products - Modify/select the savings products that are to be attached to this credit type.

11\. When done click on the UPDATE Button to complete this task.

On successful completion you will get a pop up message confirming that the operation was successful.
