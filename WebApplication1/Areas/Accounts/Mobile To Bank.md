Mobile To Bank

Use this sub-module to reconcile or view mobile to bank transactions.

To access this window, click :Accounts Module - > Operations - > Customers Account - > Mobile To Bank

The following screen will be opened:

mobile to bank fig 1.

From here you can view all transactions by selecting date range,you can search .

Mobile To Bank Fig2

Auto-Matched-These are transactions which the system matched with correct account automatically which means mobile number and account number were matching .

Recon-Matched-These are transactions which were matched by system user .This means they were not completed successfully.

N/B -For you to reconcile just click on this icon select the correct account and product then click on Update button

Unmatched - these are transactions which have not been matched to the correct accounts.
