How to create a Savings Product

Click on the CREATE Button, to open the following screen:

Savings Fig.1

On this Create screen, fill in all the required data in all fields:

Name - Enter the name of the Savings Product you want to create.

Maximum Withdrawal - Enter the maximum amount that can be withdrawn by a customer per transaction.

Minimum Balance - Enter the Minimum balance that cannot be withdrawn by the customer unless he/she is closing down the account.

Operating Balance - Enter the minimum operating balance (balance that can attract interest earning for the customer)

Withdrawal Notice Amount- Enter the amount that a customer needs to give a notice before withdrawing.

Withdrawal Notice Period (Days)- Enter the number of days that the notice will take.

Withdrawal Interval (Days)- Enter the interval days that a customer should withdraw (if applicable)

Annual Percentage Yield-Enter the interest percentage to be earned annually.

Recovery Priority - Enter the recovery priority to be enforced upon the product being created. ie 1,2,3...

G/L Account - Click on the lookup icon and select the appropriate G/L account for the savings product.

When done click on the CREATE Button to complete this task.

On successful completion you will get a pop up message confirming that the operation was successful.

How to Edit a Savings Product

1\. First Identify the savings product to be edited.

Savings fig2.

2\. Alongside that savings product that's been identified, at the far left, double click on the edit icon and edit appropriately.

3\. On this Edit screen, modify the relevant fields:

Name - Modify the name of the Savings Product

Maximum Withdrawal - Modify the maximum amount that can withdrawn by a customer.

Minimum Balance - Modify the Minimum balance that cannot be withdrawn by the customer unless he/she is closing down the account.

Operating Balance - Modify the minimum operating balance (balance that can attract interest earning for the customer)

Withdrawal Notice Amount-Modify the amount that a customer needs to give a notice before withdrawing.

Withdrawal Notice Period (Days)- Modify the number of days that the notice will take.

Withdrawal Interval (Days)- Modify the interval days that a customer should withdraw (if applicable)

Annual Percentage Yield-Modify the interest percentage to be earned annually

Recovery Priority - Modify the recovery priority to be enforced upon the product being created. ie 1,2,3...

Charge borne By - Modify appropriately to be either customer or institution, depending on the entity in focus.

G/L Account - Click on the lookup icon and modify by selecting the appropriate G/L account.

Under the second bar of Well Known Charges

Select the charges attached to that product and modify accordingly

4\. When done click on the UPDATE Button to complete this task.

On successful completion you will get a pop up message confirming that the operation was successful.
