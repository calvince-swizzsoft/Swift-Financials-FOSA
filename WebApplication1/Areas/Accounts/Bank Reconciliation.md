Periods

Periods

Use this sub-module to create period which you want reconcile within a certain financial year.

To access this window, click :Accounts Module - > Operations - > Bank Reconciliation- > Periods

The following screen will be opened:

bank reconciliation Fig1.

1.To create period click On CREATE button following screen will be opened.

bank reconciliation Fig 2.

Fill in the following

a) Posting Period : Select on Financial year that you want to reconcile by clicking on look up icon

b) Bank : Select on Bank year that you want to reconcile by clicking on look up icon

Processing

Processing

Use this sub-module to process period which you have created to reconcile.

To access this window, click :Accounts Module - > Operations - > Bank Reconciliation- > Processing

The following screen will be opened:

Bank Reconciliation Fig 1.

How to process bank reconciliation

1.Click on posting period look up icon to select period that you want to reconcile

Bank Reconciliation Fig 2.

2.Select period which status still open as shown above

Bank Reconciliation Fig 3.

3.The following window will appear after selecting posting period.

a) Bank : This is the bank that you want to reconcile against

b) Bank Branch : This is the branch of the bank that you want to reconcile against

c) G/L Account Name : Name of G/L account that you want reconcile

d) G/L Account Balance : Actual of G/L account balance that you want reconcile

e) Bank Account Balance : Actual of bank account balance that you want reconcile

f) Bank Account Number : Account number of bank account that you want reconcile

g) Date : date range that you want to reconcile

h) Remarks : Remarks that describe reconciliation

Bank Reconciliation Fig 4.

4.Unreconciled balance is adjusted G/L Account Balance plus adjusted Bank Account Balance.The case above shows that bank balance is greater than G/L Account balance.It might be a result of unrepresented cheques.

Bank Reconciliation Fig 5

5.There are scenario where cheque has been written and presented to the bank and it has been cleared but its missing in the G/L Account what you do is to credit G/L Account and debit Bank Account with the same figure as shown above .

Bank Reconciliation Fig 6

6 .There are scenario when cheque has been been presented to the bank and it has affected G/L Account what you do is to Debit G/L Account and Credit Bank Account with the same figure as shown above .

Bank Reconciliation Fig 7

7\. In the above scenario bank charges some fees like withdrawal charges but not affected in G/L Account you debit bank account and credit expense G/L Account meant for bank charges.You click on ADD button to populate grid as shown above .

Bank Reconciliation Fig 8

8\. In the above scenario bank pays dividends and it has not been captured in G/L Account ,you credit bank account and debit income G/L Account meant for dividend from bank.You click on ADD button to populate grid as shown above .

Remember to click on add to reconcile any mismatches or remove to get rid of any erroneous mismatches in the reconciliation process, for every task carried out.

Closing

Use this sub-module to close period which you have reconciled .

To access this window, click : Accounts Module - > Operations - > Bank Reconciliation - > Closing

The following screen will be opened:

Bank Reconciliation Closing Fig1.

- 1. Select the posting period by clicking on look up icon

Bank Reconciliation Fig2.

2\. Check whether every detail is correct then insert remarks and select either post or reject depending on the decision that you have made then click on UPDATE button.

Bank Reconciliation Fig3

3\. Message will pop up when unreconciled balance is not zero so you must reconcile to zero balance

On successful completion you will get a pop up message confirming that the operation was successful.
