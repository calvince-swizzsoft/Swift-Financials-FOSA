Intra-Account Transfer

Use this sub-module to transfer money from customer accounts i.e from savings to repay loan or from investment to savings of same individual

To access this window, click :Accounts Module - > Operations - > Customers Account - > Intra-Account Transfer

The following screen will be opened:

intra account transfer figure 1.

How to create Inter-Account Transfer

1.Click on customer look up icon to pick origination account

Inter-Account Transfer Fig2

2.Search account number then specify the origin of funds like highlight above

3.Select the destination account by again clicking customer look up icon

Inter-Account Transfer Fig3

4\. All accounts under the parent account will appear just like Inter-Account Transfer Fig3 above then you select destination of funds Intra - Account Transfer Fig4

5.Ensure that there is enough money to transfer by checking available balance at far top right corner, type amount to transfer then narrate both primary and

secondary description.In case of loan principal and interest balance will appear then you indicate amount to clear it can be less than or equal to but not greater than the balance.

If interest is zero kindly go to Accounts Module - > Operations - > Recurring Procedures - > Capitalize Interest and charge interest for that specific loan.

6.You can effect charges by ticking

7.If there is any carry forward in the account tick this part to recover

7.Click on button ADD .You can transfer money from single accounts to various products

Click on UPDATE button

On successful completion you will get a pop up message confirming that the operation was successful.
