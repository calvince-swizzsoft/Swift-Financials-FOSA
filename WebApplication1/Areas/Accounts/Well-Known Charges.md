Well-Known Charges

- Well known charges

These are charges which are regularly applied in Financial Institutions.They are preset in the system.

We use this Module to link these charges to their corresponding charges that we had earlier created.

To access this module,

click on Accounts->Setup->Levies & Charges-> Charge Determination-> Well-Known Charges.

Well-Known Charges

Well known charges fig.2

- Deceased Membership Termination Processing Fee - This is the fee a customer bears in the event that a member passes away, and his membership has to be terminated.
- National Hospital Insurance Fund (NHIF)Fee - This is the fee attached to the medical cover provided by the NHIF body which a customer bears in every payout.
- Normal Membership Termination Fee - Refers to the fee a member bears if he decides to terminate his membership through the normal membership termination process.
- National Social Security Fund (NSSF)Fee - Refers to the fee a customer bears when registered with the NSSF body on every pay out.
- Pay As You Earn (PAYE) Fee - This is the fee a member pays according to his/her earnings on every payout.
- Express Membership Termination Fee - Refers to the fee a member bears if he decides to terminate his membership through the express membership termination process.
- Provident Fund Fee - This is the fee a member bears when contributing to the retirement savings program according to stipulated policies.
- Retiree Membership Termination Processing Fee - This refers to the fee a retired member bears when processing his/her membership termination through the retiree membership termination process.
- Voluntary Membership Termination Processing Fee - This refers to the fee a member bears when he/she decides to voluntarily withdraw his membership from the sacco.
