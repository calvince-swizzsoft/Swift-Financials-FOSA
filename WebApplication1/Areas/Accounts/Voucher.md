Journal Vouchers

Use this sub-module to create batch Journal Vouchers.

These are forms used to process accounting entries and are primarily used for fund transfers and corrections.

To access this window, click :Accounts Module - > Operations - >Batch Procedures - >Batch Origination- > Voucher.

To view the available journal vouchers, select the correct start and end dates from the calendar icons.

Select the status of the voucher you want to view from the drop down menu; then click on the refresh button to reload data onto the screen as shown above.

How to create a Journal Voucher Entry

1\. To create a Journal Voucher Entry, click on the CREATE Button. The following screen will open:

Journal Voucher fig2.

2.On this screen;

Type - Click on the arrow pointing downwards, to reveal the hidden drop down menu items to select whether it's a Debit or a Credit on the Bulk GL Account. See below;

G/L Account - Click on the lookup icon to open either a "G/L Account Lookup" or a "Customer Account Lookup" depending on the type of Journal Voucher you're creating.

Posting Period - Click on the posting period Look up icon and select the Financial year of the journal voucher.

Reference - Type the reference in accordance with the journal voucher being created.

Value date - Enter the date of the journal voucher.

Principal - Enter the principle amount

2\. On the Voucher detail pane

Type - Select from the drop down list the type of account to be affected, either G/L Account or Customer Account.

G/L Account # - Click on the lookup icon to search for the corresponding G/L account of the journal voucher.

Branch # - Click on the lookup icon to tag the account branch.

Primary & Secondary Descriptions - Enter brief descriptions relevant to the JV that you are creating.

Reference - Type the reference in accordance with the journal voucher being created.

Principal - Enter the principle amount to be distributed.

In case the the journal voucher involves a loan account the interest part will be shown.

REMEMBER: Click on the "ADD" Button to add this type of transaction into the system.

Repeat this steps until you exhaust the principal amount.

3.To complete the task click on the CREATE Button.

On successful completion you will get a pop up message confirming that the operation was successful.

How to view the Journal Voucher Entries

1\. Click on REFRESH Button to reload the data.

2\. Identify and select by clicking on the Journal Voucher that's to be authorized.

3\. To view the Journal Vouchers that have been Posted, Rejected or Pending; at the bottom of that screen on the left side you will find drop down status Button.
