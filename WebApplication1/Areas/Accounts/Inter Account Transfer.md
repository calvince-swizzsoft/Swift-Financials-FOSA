Inter-Account Transfer

This module is used to transfer money from savings to repay loan ,savings to boost shares or shares to increase savings.

It can also transfer money to G/L accounts against customer accounts and vice verser but for the same customer.

To access this window, click :Accounts Module - > Operations - > Batch Origination- > Inter-Account Transfer

The following screen will be opened:

How to create an inter account transfer.

Click customer look up icon to select the correct customer's account

1.Select accounts which you are transferring money from . From the figure above account can either be Ordinary Savings(FOSA) or Akiba Savings

2.On apportionment select weather its customer account or G/L account

3.Click on look up icon to select either customer account or G/L account depending on what you apportion to.

4\. If it is a loan account, the principal and interest balance will appear ,you can only clear amounts equal to or less than but not greater than the balance the member is having.

5 Select on indefinite charges if they apply to the case you are handling.Check the figure below :

6.Give both Primary and Secondary description which will appear on customer statement then click on ADD button then click on UPDATE button in case of single transaction.

On successful completion you will get a pop up message confirming that the operation was successful.
