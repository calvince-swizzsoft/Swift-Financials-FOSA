Text Alert Charges

- Text Alert Charges

Use this sub-module to set the unknown charges (charges not known by the system), for example setting cash withdrawal charges such that for every cash withdrawn a customer will have to bear some charges for the alert sent.

To access this module:

Accounts Module - > Setup - > Levies & Charges - > Charges Determination - > Text Alert Charges

The following screen will be opened:

Text Alert Charges fig.1

1\. Select on which transaction you want to link charge e.g cash withdrawal and then tick on applicable charges like SMS Notification Charge and then click on Update button.

System Transaction Code :

- ABC Bank - the customer will receive a chargeable text alert for any transaction carried out using the ABC Banking environment.
- Agency Banking - the agent will receive a chargeable text alert for every transaction carried out using the agency banking environment.
- Archive Balance - the customer will receive an alert(chargeable) about his/her archived balance.
- Cash Receipt (Customer) - the customer will receive an alert about the cash received into his/her account.
- Bank Reconciliation - the customer will be notified through a chargeable alert about reconciliation of his/her Sacco and bank accounts.
- Bank to Treasury - the institution will be notified about the transactions from the bank to the treasury.
- Cash Deposit (Customer)- the customer will receive an alert about cash deposited into his/her account.
- Cash Withdrawal(Customer) - the customer will receive an alert about cash withdrawn from his/her account.
- Cash Withdrawal(Account Closure) - the customer will receive a notification about the cash withdrawal transaction in particular reference to account closure.
- Payment Voucher - the customer will receive a notification about payment voucher(s) he/she has used to make any payments.
- Cheque Deposit (Customer)- the customer will receive a notification about a deposited cheque into his/ her account.
- Citius - the customer will receive a notification about any transaction carried out in the citius environment.
- Cash Pickup Batch - the customer will be notified about the cash pickup transaction originated from his/her account.
- Check-Off Batch - the customer will be notified about the check-off batch transaction carried out in his/ her account.
- Pay out Batch - the customer will be notified about the pay out batch transaction originated from his/her account.
- Sundry Payment Batch - the customer/institution will be notified about the sundry payment transaction carried out through their accounts.
- Debit Batch - the customer/institution will be notified about the debit batch originated from their accounts.
- e-Statement - the customer will receive a notification about the e-statements sent to his/her mail or through text message notification.
- Expense Payables - the customer/ institution will receive a notification about expense payables originated from their respective accounts.
- External Cheque - the customer/institution will be notified about an external cheque received into the bank/ Sacco.
- Fiscal Period Closing - the institution will be notified about the fiscal period closing when a year comes to an end.
- Fixed Deposit - the customer will receive a notification about a fixed deposit transaction carried out through his/ her account.
- Cash Payment (Sundry)- the customer/institution will receive a notification about the sundry cash payment carried out through their accounts.
- Cash Receipt(Sundry)- the customer/ institution will be notified about a sundry cash receipt into their accounts.
- Cheque Receipt (Sundry)- the customer/ institution will receive a notification about a sundry cheque receipt into their respective account(s).
- General Ledger - the institution will be notified about a transaction carried out through their general ledger account(s).
- Guarantor Attachment - the customer will receive a notification about a guarantor attached to his/ her loan product.
- Guarantor Relieving - the customer will receive a notification about a guarantor relived from his/ her loan product.
- Guarantor Substitution - the customer will receive a notification about guarantor substitution on his/her loan product.
- In-House Cheque - the customer/ institution will be notified about any transaction carried out in particular reference to in-house cheques.
- Inter-Account Transfer - the customer / institution will be notified about an inter-account transfer carried out in their respective accounts.
- Interest Capitalization - the customer will receive a notification about interest capitalization transaction(s) carried out on his/ her account and/or loan product.
- Intra-Account Transfer - the customer/ institution will receive a notification about an intra-account transfer transaction carried out on his/ her account.
- Balance Adjustment - the customer will receive an alert about a balance adjustment on his/her loan product.
- Balance pooling - the customer will receive an alert about balance pooling transaction carried out on his/ her account.
- Journal Voucher - the customer/ institution will receive an alert about any operations carried out on journal voucher entries in particular reference to the respective accounts.
- Legacy Balance -
- Loan Disbursement - the customer will be notified about a loan amount disbursed to their accounts.
- Loan Guarantee - the customer will be alerted about a loan guarantee enforced upon his/ her loan product.
- Loan Charges - the customer will be notified about loan charges that will be deducted from a loan product offered to him/ her.
- Loan Offsetting - the customer will be notified about a loan offsetting transaction carried out on his/her account in reference to a loan product hs/she has been offered.
- Loan Restructuring - the customer will be notified about restructuring operations carried out on his/her loan product.
- MCo-op Cash - the customer/ institution will be notified about any transactions carried out on the MCo-op Cash banking environment.
- Membership Termination - the customer will be notified about the membership termination operations being carried out on their accounts.
- Mobile to Bank - the customer/institution will be notified about any transaction carried out in the mobile to bank environment.
- Refund Batch - the customer/institution will receive a notification about a refund batch transaction carried out on their accounts.
- PesaPepe - the customer/ institution will be notified about any transaction carried out on the PesaPepe environment.
- PesaPepe Microloan - the customer /institution will be notified about any transaction carried out on the PesaPepe environment in particular reference to microloans.
- Sacco-Link - the customer/ institution will receive a notification about any transaction carried out in the SaccoLink environment.
- Salary - the customer will be receive a notification every time their salary is disbursed into their account(s).
- Savings Charges - the customer will receive a notification about the savings charges that will be deducted from their savings accounts.
- SkyWorld - the customer/ institution will receive a notification about any transaction carried out on the SkyWorld environment.
- Sparrow - the customer/ institution will receive a notification about any transaction carried out on the Sparrow environment.
- SpotCash- the customer/ institution will receive a notification about any transaction carried out on the SpotCash environment.
- Standing Order - the customer will receive a notification about any standing order enforced upon their account(s).
- Statement fee - the customer will receive an alert about statements fees charged on his/her accounts for the statements sent.
- Teller Cash Transfer - the institution will be notified about any cash transfer carried out by the teller.
- Teller End of Day - the institution will be notified about the teller end of day operations and everything related to it.
- Treasury to Bank - the institution will be notified about any transaction carried out between the treasury and the bank.
- Treasury to Teller - the institution will be notified about any transaction carried out between the treasury and the teller.
- Treasury to Treasury - the institution will be notified about any transaction carried out between the treasury and the treasury.
- Truncated Cheque - the customer/ institution will be notified about any transactions carried out on a cheque with particular reference to a truncated cheque.
- Unclassified - the customer/institution will receive a notification about any unclassified transactions carried out within their environment of operation.
- Wire Transfer Batch - the customer/ institution will receive a notification about any electronic fund transfers carried out within their scope of operations.

NOTE : All the above notifications/ alerts are chargeable and all the applicable charges are attached to them during set up..

The charges are either borne by the customer or the institution; but in some cases, the charges can be borne by both the customer and institution as well.

On successful completion you will get a pop up message confirming that the operation was successful.
