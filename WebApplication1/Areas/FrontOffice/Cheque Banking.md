Cheque Banking

Use this sub module to bank external cheques.

To Access this sub module Click on Front-Office - > Navigate -> Operations -> Cheques -> External -> Banking

The following screen will be opened:

External Cheque Banking Fig.1

1\. Select the bank to deposit the cheque(s) by clicking on the Lookup icon to open a "Bank Linkage Lookup" dialogue box. Select appropriate the Bank where the cheques are to be banked. The bank branch and G/L Account details will be loaded automatically when you select a bank account.

2\. Click on the REFRESH Button to load the the cheques which are not yet banked onto the screen.

3\. To bank the cheques, select by checking (putting ticks) against the cheques that are to be banked.

4\. Click on the update button and the cheque(s) will be banked.

Use by (putting a tick) to select all the cheques and update.

On successful completion you will get a pop up message confirming that the operation was successful.
