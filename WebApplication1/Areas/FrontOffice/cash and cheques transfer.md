Cash Transfer

The Cash Transfer sub module is used by Tellers to transfer cash to Treasury whenever they exceed the insured maximum limit the Teller is supposed to hold at the till.

This option is also used for inter teller cash transfer.

To access this sub module:Front Office Module -> click on Navigation -> Operations -> Teller -> Cash Transfer

Cash Transfer

Cash Transfer Fig.1

The Left panel of the transfer cash window there are two radio buttons giving you an option of how you prefer to enter the data that is either through Tally-by-count or Tally-by-Total.

Click on the reset button to clear the amount entered and enter it again.

Type in a reference related to the type of transaction you are doing

The right hand side panel (parameters panel), we have already computed figures showing you all transactions in total for that day:

- Opening Balance: This is the balance that you started with
- Total Receipts: Total amount of the cash you received that day
- Total Payments: This figure shows the total amount of cash that you paid out for that day
- Expected Cash: This is the amount of cash that you have at hand
- Cheques pending transfer: If there are any uncleared cheques for that day, the total figure will be displayed here.
- Closing Balance: This shows the balance that you should have at the end of the day.

NOTE: Before clearance it shall be indicated as a SHORTAGE, once Balance It will be indicated as BALANCED, and in case you have entered excess cash, it shall be indicated as EXCESS.

Remember to click on the correct transaction type depending on the places you want to transfer cash to.( circled in red on the image below)

Cash Transfer

To compete the Cash Transfer transaction click on the SAVE Button.

On successful completion you will get a pop up message confirming that the operation was successful.

Cheques Transfer

Cheques Transfer

This module is used by the tellers to transfer cheques received during the day to the treasury.

To open this module click on Front Office -> Navigate -> Operations -> Teller -> Cheque Transfer

Cheques Transfer

Cheque Transfer Fig.1

To see the cheques received you must have the rights and privileges of a teller.

Click on the refresh buttons to see the received cheques if there are any in the system.

Select the cheque(s) that you want to transfer.

Use toggle selection to select all cheques at a go if that is what is required.

Click on the update button to transfer the cheques.
