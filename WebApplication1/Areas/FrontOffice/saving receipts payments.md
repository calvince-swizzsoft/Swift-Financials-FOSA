Cash Withdrawal

Cash withdrawal option is for withdrawing cash from a customer's account.

To access this module ,Click on Navigate -> Operations -> Teller -> Savings Receipts/Payments -> Cash Withdrawal

The screen below opens when you click on the front office module

Cash Withdrawal Fig.1

NOTE: While on this same window (above figure); take note of the "Transaction Type" field. Make sure it reads the Transaction you are doing at the moment (Cash Withdrawal).

1\. Search for customers details by clicking on the account lookup icon .

The following "Customer Account Lookup" pop up dialogue box will be opened:

Cash Withdrawal Fig.2

2\. Search by either name,identity card number,payroll number or employee number and click on the REFRESH Button.

Remember to select either Investment, Loan or Savings accounts as you search for the customer account.That menu is circled in red as show on the image above

3\. Select Ordinary Savings Account

After the customer search the system takes you back to the cash withdrawal window to let you enter the necessary details.

Cash Withdrawal Fig.3

4.Enter withdrawal amount

5.Amount the withdrawal reference

6.Remember to look through the other buttons next to the transaction button.

Teller G/L Account Statement : This option Shows all the tellers transactions performed in specific period of time

customer Account Preview - this button has other buttons that shows a customers mini statement,signatories and any uncleared cheques

Customer Preview - This button has other buttons that shows individual particulars,address and specimen (passport,signature and identity card).

Cash Withdrawal Fig.4

7\. After previewing the Customer Account now you can go ahead and grant the withdrawal request to the customer.

In the "Amount" Field Enter the amount being withdrawn by the customer.

8\. In the "Reference" Field type in anything in relation to that withdrawal for example, a reference number, or even the voucher number etc.

9\. To finalize the transaction click on SAVE Button and a receipt will be printed to that effect.

Cheque Deposit

Cheque deposit module is for receiving customer's cheques.

This module receives,local,upcountry or foreign cheques

To access this sub-module,Click on Navigate -> Operations -> Teller -> Savings Receipts/Payments -> Cheque Deposit.

The following window will be opened after you click on the front office module.

Cheque Deposit Fig.1

NOTE: While on this same window (above figure); take note of the "Transaction Type" field. Make sure it reads the Transaction you are doing at the moment (cheque deposit).

1\. Search for customers details by clicking on the account lookup icon .

The following "Customer Account Lookup" pop up dialogue box will be opened:

Cheque Deposit Fig.2

2\. Search by either name,identity card number,payroll number or employee number and click on the REFRESH Button.

Remember to select either Investment, Loan or Savings accounts as you search for the customer account.That menu is circled in red as show on the image above

3\. Select Ordinary Savings Account as shown above (circled in red).

After the customer search the system takes you back to the cheque deposit window to let you enter the necessary details.

Cheque Deposit Fig.3

4\. Once the data has been loaded, right below that data there are THREE Buttons;

- Transaction, - This Button shows Transaction Button; gives you the Transaction Types
- Teller G/L Account Statement - this Button shows the Teller's G/L account Statement Preview
- Customer Account Preview - This Button shows a preview of the Customers Account
- Customer Preview - This button has other buttons that shows individual particulars,address and specimen (passport,signature and identity card).

NOTE: Make SURE that you select the "Transaction Button" to continue with the cheque deposit process

5\. Select the "Transaction Type" field to read Cheque Deposit.

6\. To fill this window:

- Input the cheque drawer details (The Drawer is the person/institution issuing the Cheque).
- Enter the date that the cheque was written as the write date.
- Fill the Bank Name, using the look up icon Select the Drawers Bank, and remember to select the branch where the Cheque has been issued from.

refer to the window below.circled in red.

Cheque Deposit Fig.4

- Select the cheque type from the "Cheque Type Lookup" dialogue box by clicking on the look up icon.
- The maturity date of the cheque will be calculated automatically by the system depending on the cheque type. Take note of this date in case the Customer inquires as per to when his/her cheque will mature.
- Enter the amount on the Cheque that is being banked.
- Enter the Cheque Number. (Usually a SIX digit number)
- Enter the reference for the cheque deposit.

Note The customers book balance changes after inputting the amount of the cheque but this money will only be available for withdrawal after the cheque matures.

7\. To complete the transaction click on the Button UPDATE and a receipt will be printed to that effect.

Payment Voucher

Payment Voucher

Use this module to make payments using cheque leafs issued in the accounts department. When a customer requests to be issued with a cheque book by the Sacco, he/she can use the cheque leafs to make various payments.

When such a cheque leaf is presented to a teller with the details of payments to be made, the teller will use this payment voucher module to make payments.

To access this sub-module,Click on Navigate -> Operations -> Teller -> Savings Receipts/Payments ->Payment Voucher.

The following window will be opened:

Payment voucher fig.1

1\. Search for customers details by clicking on the account lookup icon .

The following "Customer Account Lookup" pop up dialogue box will be opened:

Payment voucher fig.2

2\. Search by either name,identity card number,payroll number or employee number and click on the REFRESH Button.

Remember to select either Investment, Loan or Savings accounts as you search for the customer account.That menu is circled in red as show on the image above

3\. Select Ordinary Savings Account as shown above (circled in red).

After the customer search the system takes you back to the Payment voucher window to let you enter the necessary details.

4\. Once the data has been loaded, right below that data there are THREE Buttons;

- Transaction, - This Button shows Transaction Button; gives you the Transaction Types
- Teller G/L Account Statement & - this Button shows the Teller's G/L account Statement Preview
- Customer Account Preview - This Button shows a preview of the Customers Account
- Customer Preview - This button has other buttons that shows individual particulars,address and specimen (passport,signature and identity card).

5\. Select the "Transaction Type" field to read payment voucher.

6\. Look up for the voucher number by clicking on the voucher number lookup icon.

Payment voucher fig.3

7\. Select the cheque book on the upper pane of the payment voucher lookup so that all the available cheque leaves/payment vouchers will be displayed under payment vouchers on the lower pane.

8\. Select the payment voucher that is being used for payments.

The voucher number and the cheque book number will be loaded automatically after selecting the payment voucher number.

9\. Enter the payee details.

10\. Enter the write date, the date of the cheque.

11\. Enter the amount that is on the cheque.

12\. Enter the reference number.

Notice how the customer balance reduces when you input the payment voucher amount.

Click on the update button.

When asked whether to proceed, click on the Ok button

Click on the Yes button so as to place a cash withdrawal authorization request. All payment vouchers must go through cash withdrawal authorization.

A message will pop up notifying you that the operation was completed successfully.
