This sub-module is for linking customers to their respective branches .

To access this module click on Registry Module - > Navigate - > Operations - > Customers - > Branch Linkage

How to Link a customer to a branch

1\. Click on the Branch lookup dialog box to open 'branch lookup' dialog box.

2\. Select branch from the list of branches available.

Branch linkage

Branch Linkage fig.2

3\. On the customer details pane, click on the customer lookup icon to open a customer lookup dialog box. Search and select the customer that you want to link to the selected branch. The rest of the customer details will be loaded automatically.

4\. Click on the update button to link the customer to the branch.
