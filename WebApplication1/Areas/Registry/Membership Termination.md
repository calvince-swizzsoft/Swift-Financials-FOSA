Approval

Approval

Use this sub-module to approve already registered membership withdrawal notifications.

To access this window, click :Accounts Module - > Operations - > Membership Withdrawals - > Approval

REMEMBER: You have to click on the REFRESH Button to load data onto the screen.

The following screen will be opened:

1\. Customer # - Click on the look up icon to open a "Withdrawal Notification Lookup" dialog box and select a member.

- Station - will be loaded automatically once the customer is selected.
- Serial Number # - will be loaded automatically once the customer is selected.
- Identity Card Number # - will be loaded automatically once the customer is selected.
- Payroll Number - will be loaded automatically once the customer is selected.
- Category - will be loaded automatically once the customer is selected.
- Remarks - will be loaded automatically once the customer is selected.

2\. Select any of these tabs to view the products and balance of the customer.

Savings Account - Shows you a summary of the members savings account.

Loans Account - Displays a members loans accounts and the Loan balances.

Investment Accounts - Displays a summary of a member's investment accounts.

Loans Guaranteed - Displays a members loans guaranteed.

3\. Approval Remarks - Enter the approval remarks.

4\. Select on the approval action or differ to reject the notice

5\. To finalize the membership withdrawal approval process, click on the UPDATE Button.

On successful completion you will get a pop up message confirming that the operation was successful.
