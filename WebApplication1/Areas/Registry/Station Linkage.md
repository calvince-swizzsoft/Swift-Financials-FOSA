Station linkage

This sub-module is for linking customers to their respective stations.

To access this module click on Registry Module - > Navigate - > Operations - > Customers - > Station Linkage

How to Link a customer to a station

1\. Click on the Station lookup dialog box to open 'station lookup' dialog box.

2\. Select a station from the list of stations available. You can also use the search field provided to search for a station. Zone, Division and Employer details will be loaded automatically as they are all inter-linked.

Once the station has been selected, a list of all customers who belong to that station will be displayed in the customers pane (as shown in the figure below).

3\. On the customer details pane, click on the customer lookup icon to open a customer lookup dialog box. Search and select the customer that you want to link to the selected station. The rest of the customer details will be loaded automatically.

4\. Click on the update button to add the customer to the station.

Removing a customer from a station linkage

1\. Click on the Station lookup dialog box to open 'station lookup' dialog box.

2\. Select a station from the list of stations available. You can also use the search field provided to search for a station.

Zone, Division and Employer details will be loaded automatically as they are all inter-linked.

Once the station has been selected, a list of all customers who belong to that station will be displayed in the customers pane (as shown in the figure below).

3\. Search and select the customer you want to remove from the station.

4\. Click on the reset button to remove the customer.
