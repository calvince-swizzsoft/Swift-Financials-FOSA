How to create a charge exemption

1.Click on the CREATE button to create a new charge exemption

2\. Enter the name of the charge exemption you want to create.

3\. Click on the charge lookup icon to open a charge lookup dialog box and select the charge from which you want to exempt customers.

4\. Add the the customers you want to exempt from the charge by selecting the customers from the customer lookup dialog box.

5\. You can remove a customer from the charge exemption by selecting customer and then clicking on Remove
