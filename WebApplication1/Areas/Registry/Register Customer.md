ii) Enter the date that the partnership was established.

i) Enter the details of the partnership members.

How to Register a New Customer - (Corporation Customer Type)

Register

Register-Corporation fig.1

2\. Fill in the details of the corporation

Corporation particulars tab

Register

Register-Corporation fig.2

i) Enter the name, registration number, Personal Identification number of the corporation.

ii) Enter the date that the corporation was established.

iii) Enter remarks accordingly.

4\. Member details

i) Click on the customer lookup icon to search and select a customer who is a member of the corporation.

ii) Enter remarks accordingly.

iii) Check if a member is a signatory of that corporation account.

iii) Click on the add button.

iv) Repeat step i)-iii) to add the other corporation members.
