Documents

Use this sub-module to upload customer documents.

To access this module click on Registry Module - > Navigate - > Operations - > Customers - > Documents

How to create a Customer's Document

1\. To create a customer document click on Documents and then click on the create button to open the following screen.

Documents

Documents Fig.1

2.Select on the type of document you want to upload i.e collateral or general document

Collateral documents -They are security documents.(documents pledged to secure repayment)

General Documents - This can be any document like a birth certificate or just any other document .

3.Click on the customer lookup icon to open a customer lookup icon to search for the customers (owner) of the document.

4\. Look up the customer's document by clicking on the Lookup Icon to open a "windows explorer" window. Browse to where you have saved the scanned document. Select th document to be uploaded.

5\. Enter the Title of the Document you are uploading.

6\. Give a short description of the document you are uploading in the Description field.

7\. To complete this task click on the CREATE button to save changes.
