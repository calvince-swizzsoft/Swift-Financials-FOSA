Salary Processing

This module is for processing salaries.

To access this module click on Human Resource Module -> Navigate -> Operations -> Salary -> Salary Processing.

Salary Processing

Salary Processing fig.1

Salary processing procedure.

1\. Select the salary period which you want to process the salary. To enter the Salary Period field: Click on the Look Up Icon to open a "Salary Period Look Up" Dialogue box.

The tax Relief amount, Maximum provident fund Relief,Maximum Insurance, Relief and Remarks will be loaded automatically according to how they were defined while creating a salary period.

Notice the four tabs circled in red and the data below them. The salary groups will appear under the salary groups tab.

2\. Select the particular salary groups that and you want to process salary for and then click on Employees to see the employees in that salary group.

One can choose to process salaries based on the salary groups, branches or departments.

Once you select a salary group, you will be able to see the employees in that particular group by selecting the employees tab. The same case for branches and departments.

If you wish to leave out a branch or a department because of one reason or the other, employees of that branch/department will not get their salaries as it will not be processed.

Note: Choosing a salary group is mandatory when processing salaries. When processing salaries of all employees/ all job groups, select all the job groups, branches and departments.

Once you confirm that you have confirmed that you have the right details for the payroll, click on UPDATE Button to process the salaries.

On successful completion you will get a pop up message confirming that the operation was successful.
