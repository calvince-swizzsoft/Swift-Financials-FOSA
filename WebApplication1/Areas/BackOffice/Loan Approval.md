Approval

Use this sub-module to appraise loans. This should be done by authorized loan officers only.

To access this sub module click on:Loan Origination Module -> Navigate -> Operations -> Application - > Approval.

After Appraisal the Loan case moves to the Approval stage

On the Approval page, the below window will open and you will be required to select a Loan case from the list of already Appraised loans.

once you click the Look up icon .The customers Loan application details will populate on the forms in Section1.

Approval

Section 2 allows the officer approving the loan, to check and confirm the amount applied, what has been recommended by the system plus system remarks, amount appraised and appraiser remarks, Loan amount and finally Total Loan+Interest amount for the customer.

You can also check for any Attached Loans and the Repayment Schedule for the loan to be approved. See below;
