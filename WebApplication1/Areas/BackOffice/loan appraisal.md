Loan Appraisal

Use this sub-module to appraise loans. This will only be done by authorized loan officers.

To access this sub module click on:Loan Origination Module -> Navigate -> Operations -> Application - > Appraisal

- - After Registration of the Loan, the next step is loan appraisal.
    - In order for loan appraisal to be done, the loan applicants file has to be sent from the Registry department after its registration to the Loaning Office (Credit Administration Office). Once the file has been dispatched to the Loans Office and received by the Loans Appraising Officer, then Appraisal can be done.
    - At this stage, the officer checks the data given by the applicant, the purpose of the loan and the repaying capacity of the applicant along with the viability of the proposal, go through the customers file from registry and offer his remarks.
    - NOTE: The file has to be received physically and through the system so that Appraisal function can be accepted.

Receiving a file by the Loans Department

- - In order to receive a file, (by the authorized Appraising Officer) click on the "Registry Module"
    - In cases where you cannot see the file, click on "REFRESH" Button to update the list. You can also search for the file in the Search Field.
    - Search for that file by either entering the account number of that customer. or membership number, to search and retrieve that file then Click 2.

On it (by putting a tick ) to select it and click "RECEIVE" Button as illustrated in the figure (File Reception Window) below.

- - You will receive a pop up message informing you that the Operation was successful.
    - The window below shows the File Reception Window.

Standing Orders

When this option is clicked, you will be able to see/view the customers Standing Orders instructions. All the standing orders instructions will be seen or displayed here.

Income History

Use this option to check on the Customers income history i.e Salary & Payouts

Loan Applications

Use this option to check on all pending or Loans in the application queue and their statuses.

Guarantors :

This option is used to aid in showing all the Guarantors for the particular loan applied. Among the details shown are the Total amount of shares and the Amount Guaranteed.

From the figure above, the Loans Officer can be able to see who are the guarantors for the loan applicant. The Guarantors details will be displayed here for review by the Officer.

Collaterals

Use this option to view other loan securities(collaterals)

Loan Accounts

This option is used to aid in showing all the details about the pending/current outstanding Loan accounts of the applicant including the name of the loan product and the amount among other details.

Loan Qualification Button

This option is used to enter some important information that will be used to evaluate/assess the loan applicant. Such Important data include; The applicants Net Income, as well as the Applicant's Income Adjustments (allowances and deductions).

The figure below shows the Loan Qualification Button when clicked:

How to fill the loan qualification form

1\. On the "Net Income" field; Enter the applicants Net Income.

2\. Next, under the "Income Adjustment Details" click on the Lookup icon to open "Income Adjustment Lookup" dialogue box and select the appropriate adjustment (allowance or a deduction) check the income,deduction pane in the above diagram

3\. After the selection of the allowance or deduction, next enter the amount (exact figure being added or deducted) at the "Amount" field.

4\. Then click on the "ADD" Button.

NOTE: You can only enter one allowance or deduction at a time. & Don't click on SAVE at this stage until you proceed to the Appraisal stage.

Loan Appraisal

Points to note from the figure above:

- System Recommendation &
- System Remarks

At this stage, the System will give it's recommendation, as well as System remarks (such as on what basis did it assess the loan applicant)

- Loan Officer Recommendation &
- Loan Officer Remarks

At this level, the Loans Officer has also a right to give his/her recommendation and in turn override the systems recommendation. Once the Loans Officer done this, he/she has to give his/her Remarks in the "Loan Officer Remarks" field indicating reasons for appraisal. Appraise or Reject the application.

1\. After assessing the applicants guarantors, loan balances, loan qualifications,the loans officer can now appraise or reject the application.

2\. To appraise or reject, click on the lookup icon at the Appraisal Remarks to open a "Loaning Remark Lookup" dialogue box.

3\. Select the appropriate remarks in regards to your decision.

4\. In view of the officers decision select the appropriate choice of either; APPRAISE, or REJECT and click on SAVE Button.

5\. You will receive a confirmation pop up message informing you that the operation completed successfully.
