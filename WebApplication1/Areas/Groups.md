How to create micro credit groups

1\. Click on the create button to create a group and its member.

The following screen will open.

Groups

Groups Fig2.

2\. Name - Click on the lookup icon to open a "Customer Lookup" dialog box. Select the customer to attach in a micro credit group.

3\. Credit Officer- Click on the lookup icon to open a "Micro-credit Officer Lookup" dialog box.select a of micro-credit officer to attach to the group.

4\. Parent Group - Click on the lookup icon to open a "Micro-Credit Group Lookup" dialog box.Select the parent group from the list.

5\. Type- Click on the drop down menu to Select the type of micro-credit group you're creating.

6\. Purpose- Type in a brief note on the purpose of this micro-credit group.

7\. Activities- Type in description of the activities the micro-credit group will be partaking in.

8\. Meeting Frequency- Click on the drop down menu to Select the frequency at which the micro credit group will be meeting. i.e weekly,monthly,semi-annual e.t.c

9\. Meeting Day Of The Week- Select from the list the day of the week the micro-credit group will be holding meetings. Click on the downward facing arrow to open a menu you may select the day from.

10\. Meeting Place- Type in the place where the micro-credit group will be meeting.

11\. Minimum Members- Using the arrows to decrease and increase the number of members by 1, select a number of minimum number of members allowed in the group. The upward arrow increases the number by 1 while the downward farrow decreases the number by 1.See the figure below

12\. Maximum members -Using the arrows to decrease and increase the number of members by 1, select a number of maximum members allowed in the group. The upward arrow increases the number by 1 while the downward arrow decreases the number by 1.

13\. Remarks-Type in remarks concerning the group.

14\. Click on the create button to finish creating the micro-credit group.

On successful completion you will get a pop up message confirming that the operation was successful

How to add members to a micro-credit group

1\. Select the micro-credit group to add members.

2\. Click on the edit button to open the following window

Groups

3\. Add members on the Member Details pane, circled in red in the above figure

i) Customer- click on the customer lookup icon to open 'Customer Lookup' dialogue box. Search and select the customer to add to the micro-credit group.

ii) Designation -Select the designation of that particular member in the group.

iii) Loan Cycle- Select the loan cycle for the member by using the downward and upward facing arrows. The upward facing arrow will increase the loan cycle value by1 while the downward facing arrow will decrease the loan cycle value by1.

iv) Remarks-Type in a brief remark about the member in this field.

4\. Click on the add button to add the member in the group members pane.

5\. To add other members repeat step 3 and 4

How to remove a member from a micro-credit group

1\. Select the micro-credit group to remove a member.

2\. Click on the edit button to open 'Edit Microcredit Group' window.

3\. From the group members pane, select the member that you want to remove from the group as shown in the figure below.

Micro Credit Groups Fig6.

4\. After selecting a member click on the remove button.

5\. Click on the update button to save changes.
